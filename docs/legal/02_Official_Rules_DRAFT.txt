4.2  All items are self-contained: every fact needed to answer an item is provided in the item. No item requires outside knowledge, trivia, professional credentials, or physical performance.
4.3  All scored items are free-response. No scored item is multiple-choice, true/false, or select-from-list.
4.4  Each item has either one predetermined correct answer or a predetermined numerical scoring formula. Scoring is applied by the platform under the locked answer key and formulas. No person exercises judgment in assigning any score.
4.5  All entrants in a round receive the same items in the same order. No random selection or ordering of items is used.
4.6  Time limits are set to allow a deliberate attempt at every item. Elapsed time is not a primary scoring input and is used only as described in Section 5.2(c).
4.7  Round dates and windows are stated above. Rounds 2 and the Final are administered at a single scheduled time. Round 1 is administered within a window; each entrant selects one start time within the window and may begin only once.
5. Scoring, Advancement, and Tie-Break
5.1  Score. Your score in a round is the sum of points earned on that round's items under the locked answer key and formulas. Blank items earn zero points. Incorrect answers earn zero points [unless a stated formula provides partial credit]. Invalid Final submissions (those that violate a stated constraint) earn zero points.
5.2  Advancement order. Advancement from Round 1 and Round 2 is determined by the following rules applied in order, and by nothing else: (a) total round score, higher advancing; (b) among entrants with identical total scores, score on the Tie-Order Subset for that round (Round 1: items [21–30]; Round 2: items [10–15]), higher advancing; (c) among entrants still identical, total elapsed time on the round measured from the first-item start timestamp to final submission, lower advancing.
5.3  Inclusion rule. If, after applying Section 5.2, more entrants are tied at the final advancing position than remaining capacity allows, all entrants tied at that position advance. No entrant is ever excluded from advancement by any random or discretionary method.
5.4  Winner. The winner is the eligible finalist with the highest valid Final score. Elapsed time is not used in the Final.
5.5  Tie-Break. If two or more finalists have identical highest Final scores to the full precision of the published formula, the Administrator will administer, to all tied finalists simultaneously and under Final conditions, the first problem from a sealed Tie-Break set prepared and committed under Section 7 before the Final. The highest score on that problem wins. If still tied, the next sealed problem is administered, and so on until a single highest score exists. If the sealed set is exhausted, an additional problem will be prepared by the original item authors under the same commitment process and administered in the same way. No coin flip, drawing, random number, or preference of any person will be used.
5.6  Certification. The Administrator certifies the advancing list after each round and the winner after the Final, based solely on Sections 5.1 to 5.5 and the eligibility and integrity determinations in Sections 8 and 9.
